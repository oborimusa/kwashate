Reviving Excellence in Kwara Hospitality

The Kwara State Hospitality and Tourism Expo (KWASHATE) returns to honor the hotels, restaurants, and individuals driving the growth of our local tourism industry.

📋 Nominate Your Business 🎟️ Reserve Your Table

Over the years, the hospitality and tourism industry in Kwara State has grown significantly. At Drecot Consults, we recognized that many firms whose works are vital to the industry were not being sufficiently rewarded. We created KWASHATE to enlighten, educate, and recognize the firms and individuals who have worked tirelessly to achieve industry-leading success.

KWASHATE is now a fully private sector initiative. We are not seeking government funding—we are seeking private sector collaboration and industry excellence.
