KWASHATE Awards

Recognizing excellence, innovation, and growth in Kwara State's hospitality and tourism industry.

🏆 Our Mission

Over recent years, the hospitality and tourism industry in Kwara State has grown significantly, and this combined with increasing customer expectation has presented many new challenges for firms across the industry.

At Drecot Consults, we recognized this, and felt that many firms whose works are vital to the industry were not being sufficiently rewarded and recognized. To rectify this, we created the Kwara State Hospitality and Tourism Expo (KWASHATE), designed to enlighten and educate the participants, reward and recognize the firms and the individuals behind them that have worked tirelessly over the past 12 months to achieve industry-leading success, client satisfaction, and market growth.

Through our staffing division, Hospitality Recruit, we also provide professional staff and training to hotels and event centers across the state.

✅ Eligibility Requirements

To ensure the integrity and fairness of the KWASHATE Awards, the following criteria apply to all nominees:

🏨 Hotels (1+ year operation, CAC registered)
🍸 Clubs & Lounges (1+ year, CAC registered)
✈️ Airlines (1+ year operation, CAC registered)
🚌 Land Transport Companies (1+ year, CAC registered)
🍔 Restaurants & Fast-Food (1+ year, CAC registered)
