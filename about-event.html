About the KWASHATE Gala

Celebrating excellence in Kwara State's hospitality and tourism industry.

🏨 Drecot Consults

Drecot Consults is a hospitality support firm that has taken a great step to develop the hospitality industry in Nigeria, starting from Kwara State and spreading high standards through the organizing of state Hospitality Awards.

Through our staffing division, Hospitality Recruit, we provide professional staff and training to hotels, restaurants, and event centers across the state.

Our mission is to increase the level of comfort and loyalty of guests, to raise demand for hospitality industry services and related spheres, and to contribute to the economic growth of the nation.

🏛️ Official Government Approval

In 2022, the KWASHATE delegation—comprising the National Orientation Agency (NOA) and the founder of KWASHATE—met with the Deputy Governor of Kwara State to formally present the event proposal. The Deputy Governor gave his official approval and blessing for the event to proceed. However, due to bureaucratic delays and the withdrawal of government funding, the event did not materialize at that time.

✅ Approved by the Deputy Governor (2022) 🏛️ Endorsed by NOA Kwara State 🏆 Proven Government-Level Proposal 🤝 Private Sector Relaunch 2026

💡 The Concept

KWASHATE was created to enlighten, educate, and recognize the firms and individuals who have worked tirelessly to achieve industry-leading success, client satisfaction, and market growth. We bring together the best minds in Kwara's hospitality sector to celebrate innovation, dedication, and excellence.
