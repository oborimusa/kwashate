About KWASHATE 2026

The Kwara Edition of the national Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards (HTEA) — celebrating the businesses and individuals driving Kwara's hospitality sector.

Organized by Drecot Consults

Drecot Consults is a hospitality support firm working to develop the hospitality industry in Nigeria — starting from Kwara State and expanding through state-level hospitality awards.

Through its divisions — including HospitalityRecruit (staffing and training) and Drecot Printing Press (event and branding production) — Drecot Consults supports hotels, restaurants, and event centers across the state.

Our mission is to raise the standard of guest experience, increase demand for hospitality services, and contribute to the economic growth of the nation.

✨ Our Vision

To become the leading hospitality and tourism awards platform in Nigeria, recognized for celebrating excellence, promoting cultural heritage, and driving economic growth — rotating to a new state each year to build a national movement.

🎯 Our Mission

To create a prestigious platform that celebrates, rewards, and empowers the champions of Kwara State's hospitality and tourism industry — from hotel owners and restaurant managers to travel companies and culinary innovators — through recognition, networking, and business growth.

💡 The Concept

KWASHATE was created to enlighten, educate, and recognize the firms and individuals who have worked tirelessly to achieve industry-leading success, client satisfaction, and market growth. We bring together the best minds in Kwara's hospitality sector to celebrate innovation, dedication, and excellence.

Our History & Stakeholder Engagement

The KWASHATE concept was first developed in 2019. In the course of building the initiative, the founding team held consultative meetings with government stakeholders — including the National Orientation Agency (NOA) and the Office of the then Deputy Governor of Kwara State — to introduce the initiative.

The founding team meeting with government stakeholders in 2019 to introduce the KWASHATE concept.

In 2026, KWASHATE is being relaunched as a fully private-sector-led event, with renewed engagement with relevant government institutions underway.
