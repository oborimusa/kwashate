Two days. Two experiences. Food Exhibition & Fair on Day 1, Awards Gala on Day 2. Reserve your place at KWASHATE 2026.

📅 Event Details

Event: KWASHATE 2026 — Kwara Edition of the Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards

Dates: Friday 4th – Saturday 5th December, 2026

Venue: Starwood Hotels O2 Arena, Ajase-Ipo Road, Ilorin, Kwara State

KWASHATE 2026 is a two-day celebration. Day 1 is the Food Exhibition & Fair — open to the public, featuring local cuisine, vendor stalls, and the live cooking challenge. Day 2 is the Awards Night & Gala — a black-tie ceremony crowning the winners of the 18 award categories and the Face Off KWASHATE finale.

Two Days. Two Experiences.
Choose your day — or join us for both.
Day 1 Food Exhibition & Fair

🍽️ A Celebration of Nigerian Cuisine

An all-day celebration of Nigerian food, culture, and culinary talent. Open to the public. Features vendor stalls, live cooking competition, local ingredient showcase, and cultural performances.

🎟️ Individual Entry [₦5,000 per person] Access to the Food Fair, tastings, and entertainment.
🏪 Vendor Booth From ₦15,000 Exhibit at the Food Fair. 3 tiers available: Standard, Premium, Signature.
Day 2 Awards Night & Gala

🏆 The Crowning Moment

A black-tie gala celebrating Kwara's hospitality and tourism champions. Features the 18-category awards ceremony, the Face Off KWASHATE finale, and an evening of fine dining and entertainment.

🎟️ Individual Ticket [₦25,000 per person] One seat at the gala dinner and awards ceremony.
💺 Table (10 seats) From ₦250,000 Standard or VIP table. See pricing below.

The Food Fair is open to the public and to the industry. Restaurants, caterers, food entrepreneurs, and vendors come together to exhibit, sell, and compete.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy a single ticket instead of a full table?

Individual tickets for Day 2 (Gala) will be confirmed at a later date. Currently, Day 2 is sold by table of 10. Day 1 (Food Fair) is open to individual entry at the door.

Is Day 1 (Food Fair) open to the public?

Yes. Day 1 is open to the public. Individual entry tickets will be available at the gate or via pre-registration.

Can I attend both days?

Yes. Many guests attend both days. You will need a separate booking for Day 1 entry and Day 2 table or ticket.

When do I pay?

After your reservation is confirmed. We will send bank details once we have verified availability.

Do you offer student rates?

Students can attend KWASHATE through Face Off KWASHATE (the professional challenge), which has its own eligibility. Learn more →

Are dietary requirements accommodated?

Yes. Please tell us about any dietary requirements when you reserve so we can inform the caterer.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes. Starwood Hotels O2 Arena has on-site parking. Details will be shared with confirmed guests before the event.

What is the dress code?

Day 1 (Food Fair): smart casual. Day 2 (Awards Gala): black tie / formal. Traditional Nigerian formal attire is also welcome at the Gala.
