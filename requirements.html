Category Requirements

The official judging criteria for every KWASHATE 2026 award category.

📋 View the full list of award categories

🏨 Hotels Category

Eligibility: Hotels operating in Kwara State for at least 1 year with valid CAC registration.

  • Best Luxury Hotel: Exceptional interior design, premium amenities (spa, pool, gym), 24/7 room service, high-end dining, and a 4.5+ star online reputation.
  • Best Green Hotel: Sustainable practices (solar energy, water recycling, waste reduction), eco-friendly materials, and community environmental programs.
  • Best Sport Hotel: On-site fitness facilities (gym, swimming pool, sports courts), proximity to sports venues, and athlete-friendly services.
  • Best Business Hotel: High-speed Wi-Fi, conference/meeting rooms, business center services, and quiet, work-friendly environments.
  • Best Design Hotel: Unique, visually stunning architecture and interior design, blending modern style with local cultural elements.
  • Best Historical Hotel: Historical significance, preservation of original architecture, and educational storytelling about the property's past.
  • Best Innovative Hotel: Use of cutting-edge technology (smart rooms, AI concierge), unique guest experiences, and creative operational solutions.
  • Opening of the Year: A hotel that opened within the last 12 months, demonstrating immediate excellence in service, design, and guest satisfaction.
  • Best Bar In A Hotel: A hotel bar with a premium drink selection, expert mixology, excellent atmosphere, and professional service.
  • Best Nightlife Hotel: A hotel that offers vibrant evening entertainment, live music, DJ events, and a safe, enjoyable nightlife experience.
  • Best Hotel GM: An individual General Manager demonstrating outstanding leadership, guest satisfaction scores, and staff development.
  • Best Guest House: A smaller property offering warm hospitality, clean and comfortable rooms, and personalized guest services.
  • Best Motel: A clean, safe, and affordable property with convenient access to major roads, offering basic but quality accommodation.
  • Best Night View Hotel: A hotel with spectacular views of the Kwara State skyline or natural landscapes, offering excellent outdoor seating areas.
  • Best Corporate Social Responsibility: A hotel actively giving back to the local community through charity, education, or environmental projects.

🍔 Restaurants & Fast-Food Category

Eligibility: Restaurants operating in Kwara State for at least 1 year with valid CAC registration.

  • Best Restaurant: A full-service restaurant offering exceptional cuisine, a welcoming ambiance, impeccable service, and high-quality locally sourced ingredients.
  • Best Fast-Food: A quick-service restaurant offering fresh, delicious meals with efficient service, clean facilities, and good value for money.
  • Best African Restaurant: A restaurant specializing in traditional Nigerian and African cuisine, celebrating local flavors and culinary heritage.
  • Best Continental Restaurant: A restaurant offering international cuisine (European, Asian, etc.) with authentic recipes and a sophisticated dining experience.

🚌 Transport Category

Eligibility: Transport companies operating in Kwara State for at least 1 year with valid CAC registration.

  • Best Transport Company: A company offering reliable, safe, and punctual transportation services with well-maintained vehicles.
  • Best Operating Airline: An airline operating from Kwara State offering excellent customer service, flight punctuality, and passenger comfort.
  • The Oldest Transport Company: A transport company with a long-standing history in Kwara State, demonstrating resilience and enduring service to the community.
  • Best Traveling Agency: A travel agency offering exceptional tour packages, customer support, and unique travel experiences in Kwara State.

🎉 General Category

Eligibility: Businesses operating in Kwara State for at least 1 year with valid CAC registration.

  • Best Club: A premier nightclub offering top-tier sound systems, lighting, DJ performances, and a safe, enjoyable atmosphere.
  • Best Lounge: An upscale lounge offering premium drinks, comfortable seating, a sophisticated ambiance, and excellent customer service.
  • Best Event Planner: An event planning company demonstrating creativity, organizational excellence, and successful execution of high-profile events.
  • Best Event Centre: A versatile event hall with modern facilities, professional staff, and a proven track record of hosting successful events.
  • Best Catering Services: A catering company offering exceptional food quality, presentation, and service for weddings, corporate events, and private parties.
  • Best Event Decorator: A decorator with a portfolio of beautifully designed events, showcasing creativity, attention to detail, and the ability to transform spaces.
