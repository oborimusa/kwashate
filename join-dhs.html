🤝 Welcome to Hospitality Recruit

Hospitality Recruit is the staffing division of Drecot Consults—a fully registered hospitality firm dedicated to providing high-level professional staff for the hospitality industry in Nigeria, starting from Kwara State and expanding across the nation.

When you join us, you’ll have the chance to pick and choose your jobs based on your schedule. This flexible schedule gives our employees the opportunity to work as much or as little as they need. For some, it's a full-time career. For others, it’s a part-time job or just extra cash.
