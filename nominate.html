Nominate Your Business

The official nomination process for KWASHATE 2026 — the Kwara Edition of the Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards (HTEA).

📋 Nomination Procedure

KWASHATE operates a Self-Nomination and Peer-Nomination system. Business owners and industry professionals are invited to submit a nomination for themselves or for a peer who has demonstrated excellence in their field.

Nominations are free. There are no nomination fees.

✅ Before You Nominate

Make sure your business meets these criteria:

  • CAC registered
  • Minimum 1 year in operation
  • Operating in Kwara State
  • Category-specific requirements (see Awards page)

Are you an individual? Face Off KWASHATE is the skills-based challenge for hospitality professionals, students, and independents. Learn more about Face Off →
