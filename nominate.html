📋 Nomination Procedure

Over 1,000 candidates enter the race to be nominated as one of the best in their category for the Kwara State Hospitality Award.

To ensure fairness and industry excellence, KWASHATE operates a Self-Nomination and Peer-Nomination system. Business owners and industry professionals are invited to submit a nomination for themselves or for a peer who has demonstrated excellence in their field.

📅 2026 Timeline

1

Submit Your Nomination

Fill out the official nomination form. You may nominate yourself or a peer in the industry.

Opens: 4th November, 2026
2

Eligibility Verification

Our team verifies that all nominees meet the eligibility criteria (CAC registration, 1+ year of operation, etc.).

Closes: 12th November, 2026
3

Public Voting Opens

Verified nominees are published on the KWASHATE website. The public casts their votes for their favorite nominee in each category.

Voting Opens: 12th November, 2026
4

Winners Announced

All category winners will be announced live at the KWASHATE Gala Event.

Gala Event: 7th December, 2026
